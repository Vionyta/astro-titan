### Summary
Deployed Tenable Proxy on-prem for all Tenable Nessus scanner to communiate with Tenable ONE (Cloud) by allowing only single outbound connectivity without creating multiple rules at customer firewall according to requirement. 3 x Nessus Scanner running on top of Tenable Core OS (based on Oracle Linux) were deployed near to the workload without passing any firewall for known and unknown credential scanning.
