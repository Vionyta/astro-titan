### Summary
Deployed 4 categeories of FireEye CyberSecurity Products which were Network Sercurity with APT, Endpoint Security (EDR), Email Security and Central Management. The entire project took 3 months to complete including the migration from the existing security products to FireEye products. Most of the time was spent on the endpoint agent deployment due to limited network bandwidth at remote sites (ADSL with 4G backup).
